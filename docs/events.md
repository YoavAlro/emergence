## Hype Waves

During a wave, hype orbs flood the Current and you can bet EP on the wave's upgrade. When it ends you
call it: **lasting shift or passing hype?** Lasting upgrades you bet on stay; passing ones evaporate and
some leave a hangover (users drift away). The recap scores your calls.
