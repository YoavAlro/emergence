# Claude lineage: research model → Claude Opus 5.5

Legend: ✅ well-established · ✅² recent, verified against the primary source listed in the game's fact card
(Sep 2026 fact pass). Nothing marked 🔎 ships. Shipped content lives in `src/config/claudeForms.ts`.

**Signature mechanic: the Constitution.** Claude's Stage 2 food is *Constitution principles*
(RLAIF, where the model critiques itself against written principles). The Constitution meter fades
without practice. Below 25 you're toxic (smog hurts 70% more); above 80 you over-refuse (30% of what you
eat is refused and users drift away). A Constitution of 50+ shrugs off jailbreakers. Later Claude recipes
keep a 10–15% share of principles.

**Consequence:** shadow-library books spawn in Stage 1–2 (they count as Books). Eating any sets a flag
that brings back the **authors' settlement** storm in Stage 5.
